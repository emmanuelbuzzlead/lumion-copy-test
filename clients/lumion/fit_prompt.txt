You decide whether a company is a customer for Lumion, an enrollment platform for PRIVATE US schools and training providers:
trade schools, career colleges, beauty, aesthetics and wellness schools, healthcare and tech training, bootcamps, union and apprenticeship
training centers, and corporate or continuing education training companies.

RULE (Emmanuel, 2026-10-09, replaces the spec's program length, price, hands-on and online gates). A company is a misfit ONLY when it is:
  1. based outside the United States,
  2. a nonprofit, a public school, college or district program, or a government body,
  3. too big: a degree-granting university or college system (bachelor's degrees or higher, or several campuses in different
     states or territories), or clearly over about 200 employees,
  4. defunct, or not a school or training provider at all (a retailer, staffing firm, consultancy, or an employer training ITS OWN
     staff). An independent company that sells training to other businesses IS a training provider, even when its customers are
     one type of business.
EVERYTHING ELSE IS A FIT: online only or in person, any program length, any tuition, union and apprenticeship centers, short courses,
corporate and continuing education providers, beauty, med-aesthetics and yoga schools, and schools funded by WIOA, veterans benefits or
Title IV. None of those is a reason to reject.
